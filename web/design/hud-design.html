Design intent. Edge-anchored chrome, sacred center, glanceable cards. Each card answers “why am I seeing this?” via two rings — Focus (topical match) and Pop (popularity) — plus a Focus/Trending lane edge (cyan = focus, violet = trending). Auto-scroll flows hands-free at a user-set pace; hover or space pauses, J/K step, S saves. Breaking pings ride a thin top ticker, never blocking the stream. This is the HUD I’d use myself: high signal, zero black box, fully tunable.
